# Community Standards

Relay is built by people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and working styles. We aim
for a community that is technically rigorous, respectful, and safe to participate in.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and kind in technical discussion.
- Assume good intent while addressing the behavior or code under review.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and security boundaries.
- Make room for different perspectives and experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, or intimidation.
- Sharing another person's private information, credentials, or proprietary code without permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussion or sabotaging the project.
- Using contribution channels to promote harmful, abusive, or unlawful activity.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to
protect the project and its community. Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/piyushptiwari1).
